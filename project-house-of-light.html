House of Light

A warm apartment interior shaped by filtered daylight, crafted timber and the owners’ collection.

Scope
Interior design
Area
2,100 sq ft
Status
Independent concept study

The idea

The original apartment was fragmented by corridors and deep rooms. Selective openings now draw daylight across the plan while preserving quieter corners for work and retreat.

Pale oak joinery links living, dining and study spaces. Lime-finished walls, woven textures and collected Indian art soften the apartment without crowding it.

This independent study demonstrates FORMA’s design approach; no commissioned client or built outcome is implied.

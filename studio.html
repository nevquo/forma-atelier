The studio

Ideas rooted in place.

We are a small design studio working closely with clients, craftspeople and landscapes to make architecture that feels inevitable—not imposed.

Our point of view

FORMA began with a simple belief: the best spaces grow from careful listening. We study light, climate, movement, materials and daily rituals before drawing an answer.

Our work is contemporary but never detached from context. From a family home in Kolkata to a coastal retreat in Goa, each project is shaped by its own conditions and made to age with grace.

The studio stays intentionally hands-on. The same people who lead the first conversation remain present through detailing, site decisions and final styling.

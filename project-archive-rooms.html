Renewal · Kolkata · 2022

Archive Rooms

A North Kolkata townhouse renewed as a cultural workspace without erasing its accumulated history.

Scope
Renewal + interiors
Area
6,400 sq ft
Status
Independent concept study

The idea

The project began with repair rather than replacement. Layers of paint were carefully removed, damaged timber was consolidated and the original mosaic floors guided the new spatial rhythm.

Contemporary lighting, services and worktables sit as legible additions. They support exhibitions, research and gatherings while allowing the old rooms to remain the main event.

This independent study demonstrates FORMA’s design approach; no commissioned client or built outcome is implied.

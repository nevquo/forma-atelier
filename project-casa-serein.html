Casa Serein

A coastal home organised around shade, breeze and the slow transition between inside and out.

Scope
Architecture + interiors
Area
4,800 sq ft
Status
Concept study

The idea

Casa Serein is imagined as a sequence of sheltered rooms gathered around water. Deep verandas temper the western sun, while openings align with prevailing coastal breezes.

A limited palette of pale concrete, local laterite, dark timber and handmade plaster gives the house a quiet material weight. Each room has a different relationship to landscape, but the architecture stays deliberately calm.

This is an independent concept project created to demonstrate FORMA’s approach; no client or built outcome is implied.

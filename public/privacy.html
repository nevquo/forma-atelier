What the form collects

When enabled, the form stores your name, email, topic, message, consent and submission date in Cloudflare D1. It returns a reference so you can identify the enquiry later.

Why it is collected

NEVQUO uses these details to review and respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sensitive personal information. This form does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Spam protection

The enabled form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Your browser contacts Cloudflare to perform a security check. The application does not store your IP address in its enquiry database.

Access and retention

Enquiries are available to the site operator through the Cloudflare account. NEVQUO should delete resolved enquiries when they are no longer needed. To ask about your information or request deletion, email hello@nevquo.com and include your enquiry reference if you have it.

Other browsing data

This package does not install advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting provider may process request information to operate and protect the service.

Email instead

If you use the email link, your message is handled by your email provider and NEVQUO’s mailbox provider rather than this form.

Scope & process

Start with the brief.
Then decide the scope.

A single room and a new building need different kinds of work. This page illustrates how a studio could make those boundaries clear.

FORMA is not an operating architectural practice. The services below demonstrate the website’s content and enquiry structure.

01

New homes

For a brief that starts with a site.

Site questions, room relationships, initial options and a developing material direction. A real appointment would also define consultant roles, approvals and technical deliverables.

Not assumed: planning consent, engineering or construction management.

02

Interiors & renovation

For spaces that need to work differently.

A review of the existing layout, storage needs, lighting and finishes. Early decisions focus on what can stay, what needs to change and how the work might be phased.

Not assumed: structural alterations or a contractor’s price.

03

Small hospitality

For a guest experience with practical demands.

Arrival, shared spaces, rooms and outdoor routes considered alongside servicing, cleaning and maintenance. The operational brief informs the design from the start.

Not assumed: licensing, operator strategy or statutory approvals.

An example process

Make the next
decision clear.

01

Listen

Establish the needs, constraints and people involved. Write down what success would mean.

02

Explore

Compare a small number of options. Explain the compromises, not just the preferred image.

03

Develop

Resolve the chosen direction with the relevant specialists. Keep a record of decisions.

04

Review

Check the work against the brief, budget and practical requirements before proceeding.

Before getting in touch

A few useful answers.

Can I commission FORMA to design a building?

No. FORMA is an independent website concept, not a registered practice. For a real building project, consult an appropriately qualified local professional.

What should a first enquiry include?

The place or website you want to change, the problem you want to solve, an approximate budget and any important dates. A short, clear note is enough to start.

Can NEVQUO create a site like this for my business?

Yes, you can discuss a website project with NEVQUO. Use the contact page to send an enquiry or visit nevquo.com.

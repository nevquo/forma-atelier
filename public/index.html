The starting point

A building is more
than its best view.

It is also the route from the door to the kitchen, the storage that keeps a room useful, and the window you open each morning.

These studies begin with those small decisions. Each looks at a different setting, with a short brief, a few design choices and a clear account of what has—and has not—been resolved.

Nila Retreat

A six-key coastal retreat where low pavilions, shaded paths and water create an unhurried rhythm.

Scope
Architecture + interiors
Area
8,200 sq ft
Status
Independent concept study

The idea

Nila Retreat breaks the guest experience into a series of low, quiet pavilions. Arrival, dining, rooms and the pool are connected by a shaded colonnade that always keeps the sea nearby.

Weathered local stone, hand-trowelled plaster and woven screens were chosen for salt-air resilience and tactile warmth. Evening lighting stays low and close to the architecture.

This independent study demonstrates FORMA’s design approach; no commissioned client or built outcome is implied.
